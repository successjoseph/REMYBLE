Project: Remy — Your Attentive Task Assistant
The Problem: We all have them: the small, repetitive tasks. "Take vitamins," "Drink water," "Stand up and stretch," "Check emails." We use to-do lists or standard reminder apps, but the notifications are passive. We swipe them away and forget them five seconds later. They're ignorable, and that's why they fail.

The Solution: Remy is a new kind of productivity app that acts as a true personal assistant. It's designed to break the "swipe-and-forget" cycle by ensuring you consciously acknowledge your tasks.

Instead of a passive notification, Remy uses an interactive, on-screen prompt that appears at just the right moments. It's built to be present, persistent, and smart.

The Remy Experience:

Intelligent Reminders: Remy doesn't just buzz at a set time. It learns your workflow and intelligently finds the perfect moment to remind you, often surfacing tasks right when you're picking up your device after a break.

Active Acknowledgement: You can't just swipe Remy away. You have to make a choice. A quick swipe marks a task as "Done," while a swipe in the other direction lets you mark it as "Doing," telling the app you're on it but not finished.

Your Personal Mascot: Remy, the rooster, is your accountability partner, helping you build powerful, consistent routines.

Core Features:

Intuitive Task Creation: Easily log your "Wakeup," "Before Bed," "Major," and "Minor" tasks.

Gamified Profile: Your personal "Reward Center." Track your streaks, earn points for consistency, and watch your progress grow.

Personalization: Choose your own avatar and customize the app with a variety of themes to match your style.

The "Pro" Experience: Remy is free to use, but the Pro Version unlocks its full potential, transforming it from a simple reminder app into a truly smart assistant.

Advanced "Focus" Mode: Remy Pro learns which apps are important to you. It will never interrupt you during deep work, gaming, or video calls.

Voice & Analytics: Get advanced analytics on your productivity and set up your reminders using just your voice.

Dynamic Theming: Unlock premium themes, including an automatic Day/Night mode that adapts to your environment.

To make it even better, we reward our most consistent free users with special opportunities to experience the Pro version, helping you build the habit first.

In short: Remy isn't just another to-do list. It's a productivity partner that actively helps you build the routines you've always wanted.
